# Privacy Policy for Cult Helper

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies about the information collected when you use the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

### The information we collect

We collect certain information from our users, this information is securely saved and protected.

The information collected includes:
- Discord user, server, channel and role IDs, and your settings for the Bot.
- IDLE FARM data the Bot sees in IDLE FARM's replies to your commands (such as your player name, workers, farms, items, claims, guild and statistics).

The Bot does not store the text of your own messages.

### How we use your information

This information is used only for the functionality of the Bot, such as reminders, calculators, rankings and statistics, and to improve the Bot. Some of it, such as your player name and worker levels, may be shown to others in the Bot's messages.
This collected information is not sold or shared with anyone else.
